# Legal and Operational Review Checklist

**Research checked:** 8 November 2026. This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice.

## Decisions required before replacing live legal pages

- [ ] Confirm the exact contracting/legal entity name, structure, registration identifiers and GST details (if applicable).
- [ ] Confirm the legal notice address and whether the public office address may be used.
- [ ] Name the grievance/privacy contact and document the request-handling process.
- [ ] Approve genuine effective dates; never backdate either document.
- [ ] Inventory production vendors, cookies, analytics IDs, data locations and contracts.
- [ ] Document retention periods for rejected enquiries, active leads, project records, invoices and analytics.
- [ ] Confirm whether marketing email/WhatsApp will be used; if yes, implement separate opt-in, records and withdrawal.
- [ ] Confirm supplier/manufacturer workflow, information shared, client approval points and due-diligence responsibility.
- [ ] Decide governing law, courts/arbitration, liability cap and consumer-law carve-outs with counsel.
- [ ] Obtain qualified Indian legal review of both drafts before publication.

## Current law research notes

- The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is official law, but commencement is phased by the 13 November 2025 Gazette notification.
- As of 8 November 2026, the provisions scheduled for one year after publication have not yet commenced; that date is 13 November 2026.
- Most substantive DPDP Act provisions listed in the notification, including sections 3–17, are scheduled for 18 months after publication (13 May 2027).
- The Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 are also phased: rules 1, 2 and 17–21 commenced on publication; rule 4 is scheduled after one year; rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 after 18 months.
- The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 remain relevant and must be assessed against actual processing.
- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 were reviewed, but applicability depends on the actual business model and transactions. Counsel should confirm applicability rather than assume it.

## Recommended commercial documents

- Master services agreement and project-specific statement of work/proposal.
- Mutual NDA where confidential concepts, formulas, supplier details or launches are discussed.
- Data-processing clauses/addendum for client personal data.
- IP assignment/licence schedule covering pre-existing tools, third-party assets, fonts, stock and portfolio rights.
- Manufacturer/supplier due-diligence checklist and direct client-supplier agreement.
- Product specification, sample approval, testing, compliance and acceptance records.
- Influencer/creator agreements, releases and advertising-disclosure requirements.
- Website maintenance/hosting terms and account-access handover record.
- Incident-response, retention/deletion and data-subject/grievance procedures.
