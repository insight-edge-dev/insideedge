# Discovery notes (Sept 2026)

## Legacy site: insideedge.info

A Framer template. Key problems:
- Stat counters render "0" (placeholder values)
- Phone **+91 70860 47498**, which conflicts with **+91 96781 53730** on insideedge.in.net
- LinkedIn/Instagram links point to **Paco Innovations**, not InsideEdge
- No clients, case studies or attributed testimonials

Conclusion: redirect it to the primary domain and stop maintaining it.

## Local / regional baseline (Guwahati, Assam)

Sampled from search results and agency sites (Brandddy, Verd Media, Assam Digital, Brand Imprint, Digital PR World, DiTech CDM, PromotEdge, Creative Reactive, Nexop Digital, Zealth, Web Infotech, Scabbard Tech, via iide.co's 2026 roundup).

Common patterns:
- "#1 / Best digital marketing agency in Guwahati" headlines, SEO-driven rather than positioning-driven
- Counter stats (clients, projects, retention), often unsourced; some render "0+"
- Six equal service cards; stock-template layouts
- Few real case studies with named clients and sourced results; no public pricing
- Contact forms and WhatsApp, but little attribution or conversion tracking

**Where InsideEdge can clearly win:**
1. Positioning ("creative that performs"), not a "#1" claim
2. Verifiable proof: named clients, sourced results
3. Editorial, art-directed design
4. Genuinely useful service pages and a real free-audit offer
5. WhatsApp-first conversion with proper tracking

## International reference points

Studios referenced for composition, typography and interaction only (not copied): BASIC/DEPT, Locomotive, Studio Freight, Cuberto, Dogstudio, Porto Rocha, AREA 17, Instrument.

Takeaways applied:
- Oversized, confident type as the main visual
- Few colours used with discipline
- Section-level theme changes for rhythm
- Editorial lists instead of card grids
- Restrained, meaningful motion (masked reveals, scroll-linked progress)
- Obvious navigation and CTAs kept intact, avoiding the usability trade-offs of award sites
