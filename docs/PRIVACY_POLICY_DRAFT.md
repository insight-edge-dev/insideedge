# Privacy Policy — Draft for Owner and Legal Review

> **Business identity:** Public-facing name confirmed as **INSIDE EDGE / InsideEdge**. The legal entity name, registration details and legal notice address are not yet confirmed.

This draft describes the intended handling of personal data on `https://www.insideedge.in.net` by InsideEdge, a creative and performance marketing agency based in Guwahati, Assam. It must be reconciled with actual operations and vendor contracts before publication.

## 1. Information we may collect

- Contact and enquiry information: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, company, website or social profile, service interest, budget, timeline and message.
- Technical and usage information: IP address, browser/device details, referring page, campaign parameters, pages viewed and interaction events, subject to the site's consent controls and configured analytics tools.
- Communications and project information supplied during later discussions or service delivery.
- Anti-abuse signals such as form timing, honeypot fields and CAPTCHA/Turnstile verification where enabled.

Do not use the website form to send sensitive personal data, payment-card data, passwords or confidential third-party information.

## 2. How information is collected

Information may be provided directly through forms, calls, email or WhatsApp; collected automatically through essential site functions and consented analytics; or supplied by a referring person where they are authorised to do so.

## 3. Purposes and grounds for processing

We intend to use information to:

- respond to enquiries and prepare proposals;
- deliver and administer agreed services;
- maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot the site;
- comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes; and
- measure and improve the site where the visitor has provided the required consent.

Submitting an enquiry authorises contact about that enquiry. It does **not** by itself authorise unrelated promotional email or enrolment in a marketing list. Any marketing programme should use a separate, specific opt-in and withdrawal process.

## 4. Cookies and analytics

The site uses essential storage for functions such as remembering cookie choices. Non-essential analytics and marketing tools are intended to load only after the corresponding consent choice. Depending on production configuration, these may include Google Tag Manager/Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity. The owner must verify the final vendor list, cookie inventory and consent behaviour before approving this policy.

## 5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared only as needed with authorised staff and service providers supporting hosting, form delivery, analytics, security or project delivery. Current intended infrastructure includes Vercel for hosting and Resend for enquiry email delivery; other tools named above apply only if configured.

For sourcing or manufacturer-coordination work, relevant project details may be shared with prospective suppliers only after the client understands the purpose and scope. InsideEdge coordinates discovery and communication; it does not represent that it manufactures products or independently guarantees a supplier's quality, compliance, delivery or commercial performance.

We do not intend to sell personal data. Disclosure may also occur when required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

## 6. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside India. Before publication, the owner must identify vendor processing locations, review contractual safeguards and confirm any restrictions or requirements under applicable Indian law.

## 7. Retention

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, legal/accounting requirements, security and dispute handling, then securely deleted or anonymised. A documented retention schedule for enquiries, analytics and client records is still required; this draft does not invent one.

## 8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are intended, including encrypted transport, restricted access, server-side form validation, anti-spam controls and security headers. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 9. Individual requests and consent withdrawal

Subject to applicable law and its commencement, individuals may request information about processing, correction, completion, erasure, grievance handling, consent withdrawal or nomination where available. Identity may need to be verified before acting. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing and may limit services that require the data.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 have phased commencement dates. Counsel must confirm which provisions are in force when this policy is approved and published.

## 10. Children

The website and services are directed to businesses and adults, not children. InsideEdge does not knowingly seek children's personal data through the enquiry forms. The owner must define an escalation/deletion process if such data is received.

## 11. External links

Links to third-party sites, maps, social services or messaging platforms are governed by those providers' own terms and privacy practices.

## 12. Changes

Material changes should be posted with a real effective date and, where required, an appropriate notice or renewed consent. Do not backdate this policy.
